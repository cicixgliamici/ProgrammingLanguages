# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background,
or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person who proposed them.
- Give feedback that is specific, actionable, and appropriate for a learning
  project.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge that contributors have different levels of
  experience.
- Credit sources and other contributors accurately.
- Accept correction gracefully and help repair mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate intimidation,
publishing private information, plagiarism, and sustained disruptive behavior
are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or
other participation that violates this policy. They may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method listed on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and
handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents this project.
